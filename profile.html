Honours and awards

BIOGRAPHY K.Porkumaran is Senior Professor and Principal, Sri Krishna College of Engineering and Technology, India. Received Bachelor’s Degree in Instrumentation and Control Engineering from Madurai Kamaraj University, India and has outstanding academic records with Master’s and Doctoral Degree in Electrical & Electronics Engineering specialized in Control Systems from PSG College of Technology. My PhD thesis titled “New Algebraic Criterion for Analysis and Design of Linear Systems” and developed a frame work and control strategies for Control Systems Engineering (Advisor: Dr. S N Sivanandam, Former Professor, PSG Tech). Have 30 years of experience in Teaching, 23 years of experience in Research and Development and 19 years of experience in Academic Administration. Professional Engineer (PEng.), Chartered Engineer (CEng.), Fellow (Institution of Engineers), Fellow (IETE), Senior Member IEEE, Senior Member ISA, Life member of Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE), Biomedical Engineering Society of India (BMESI) and Association for Computing Machinery (ACM), Past Chairman, IEEE Madras Section, Vice Chair, Sustainable Development Activities, IEEE India Council, Mentor for professional chapters like IEEE, ISOI, ISTE, BMESI. Published more than 160 research papers and 22 book chapters, extensively in international Journals and forums with good number of citations, 14 Ph.D scholars completed their Ph.D, 3 in progress, 12 patents published and four patents granted. Handled a number of industry/government funded projects and schemes. Served as editor and reviewer for many National and International Conferences and Journals of high repute. Executive Director of Armor Grandeur Private Limited- Defence Startup. My research areas of interest include Control Systems, Bio medical Engineering, AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Network, Image and Video Processing, Control Systems, Process Dynamics and Control, Modeling and Instrumentation System Design, Automation and EV, Battery Management System. I rendered my service as Academic, Governing Council Member, Convener, Member of Board of Studies for various reputed Institutions and Universities.
