THE WORK IN CONTEXT

Autonomous power for an underwater vehicle

The iDEX challenge asks for a suitable diesel alternator to start, operate and shut down autonomously while charging a lithium-ion battery bank. Dr. Porkumaran's LinkedIn project description identifies the intended application as an extra-large unmanned underwater vehicle (XLUUV).

Armor Grandeur reported demonstrating a scaled-down battery-management model during a technical review with Indian Navy representatives. A later iDEX/DIO startup spotlight says its battery management system and controller cleared Navy trials. The ₹90 lakh figure is reported in the supplied 2026 profile.
