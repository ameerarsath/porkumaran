What this research examined

G. Gopu, R. Neelaveni and K. Porkumaran proposed a non-invasive electrogastrogram (EGG) recording system to examine electrical activity of the stomach. The study treats EGG as a preliminary investigation of digestive disorders.

Method

Skin electrodes recorded gastric signals. The proposed setup conditioned and digitised the signals, sent them to a computer, and used LabVIEW to observe frequency and amplitude before and after meals. The paper reports recordings from more than 100 patients.

Reported observations

The authors compared patterns associated with dyspepsia, stomach ulcer, nausea and vomiting with a normal rhythm of about three cycles per minute. They reported differences in frequency and amplitude and proposed further signal analysis. The paper does not establish a clinical diagnostic accuracy rate.
